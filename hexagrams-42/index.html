HEXAGRAM 42 / 64

Increase

Growth, contribution, beneficial change

Wind over Thunder · Traditional trigram arrangement

Increase depicts a favourable movement in which resources, support or opportunity can expand. Growth is most beneficial when it strengthens the whole system and reaches those who can use it well. Opportunity creates responsibility as well as advantage.

Core Meaning

Increase depicts a favourable movement in which resources, support or opportunity can expand. Growth is most beneficial when it strengthens the whole system and reaches those who can use it well. Opportunity creates responsibility as well as advantage.

Invest in people, share knowledge and direct gains toward lasting capability. Avoid uncontrolled expansion, short-term gain at others' expense and assuming every increase is automatically beneficial.

Guidance for the Present Situation

Invest in people, share knowledge and direct gains toward lasting capability. Avoid uncontrolled expansion, short-term gain at others' expense and assuming every increase is automatically beneficial.

Consider what is actually happening rather than what you fear or hope will happen. Separate facts from assumptions, identify the part you can influence and choose an action proportionate to the circumstances. This hexagram is most useful when its symbolism becomes a practical question about conduct, timing and responsibility.

Work, Decisions and Relationships

Growth, contribution, beneficial change can be applied to professional choices, personal commitments and relationships by examining how people, resources and circumstances interact. Look for the pattern described by the hexagram rather than treating it as a literal forecast.

In work, review goals, capacity, timing and the effect of your decisions on others. In relationships, consider trust, boundaries, reciprocity and communication. A balanced interpretation should include both opportunity and risk.

Questions for Reflection

1. Which part of this hexagram most closely describes my present circumstances?

2. What is within my influence, and what requires patience, support or acceptance?

3. What is one clear action I can take without forcing an outcome?

About this interpretation

This is an original contemporary English interpretation inspired by traditional I Ching symbolism. A complete consultation also considers the question asked, any changing lines and the relating hexagram. The I Ching is a tool for reflection, not a guaranteed prediction or a substitute for professional advice.
