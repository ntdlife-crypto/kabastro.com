HEXAGRAM 07 / 64

The Army

Organisation, discipline, collective effort

Earth over Water · Traditional trigram arrangement

The Army symbolises coordinated strength under responsible leadership. A group becomes effective when its purpose is clear, roles are understood and discipline is fair. Authority without responsibility creates resentment; force without organisation creates disorder.

Core Meaning

The Army symbolises coordinated strength under responsible leadership. A group becomes effective when its purpose is clear, roles are understood and discipline is fair. Authority without responsibility creates resentment; force without organisation creates disorder.

Define the mission, assign responsibilities and establish consistent standards. Lead through competence and example, not intimidation. Avoid micromanagement, unclear command and treating people merely as instruments.

Guidance for the Present Situation

Define the mission, assign responsibilities and establish consistent standards. Lead through competence and example, not intimidation. Avoid micromanagement, unclear command and treating people merely as instruments.

Consider what is actually happening rather than what you fear or hope will happen. Separate facts from assumptions, identify the part you can influence and choose an action proportionate to the circumstances. This hexagram is most useful when its symbolism becomes a practical question about conduct, timing and responsibility.

Work, Decisions and Relationships

Organisation, discipline, collective effort can be applied to professional choices, personal commitments and relationships by examining how people, resources and circumstances interact. Look for the pattern described by the hexagram rather than treating it as a literal forecast.

In work, review goals, capacity, timing and the effect of your decisions on others. In relationships, consider trust, boundaries, reciprocity and communication. A balanced interpretation should include both opportunity and risk.

Questions for Reflection

1. Which part of this hexagram most closely describes my present circumstances?

2. What is within my influence, and what requires patience, support or acceptance?

3. What is one clear action I can take without forcing an outcome?
